How to read the evidence

  • Quote IDs are Reddit item IDs from the coded dataset, followed by the code applied and whether the source was an original post (OP post) or a comment.
  • Comment counts are comment items. Author IDs were not in the export, so they are not unique commenters.
  • Stages 1 and 6 draw on the same theme (V2_T4), so their counts match. Stage 3 shows the sleepiness and body-boundary count, with posture and setup codes as added support.
  • Quotes show user need. They are not clinical, spiritual, or efficacy claims.

Limits

  • Reddit data comes from self-selected participants and does not represent all meditation users.
  • This is a journey map, not a persona set, and it shows no emotion curve because emotions were not coded.
  • Clinical, trauma and mental-health readings stay descriptive and cautious.
  • Validate product decisions with Fnydox target users through concept tests, usability tests and interviews.
